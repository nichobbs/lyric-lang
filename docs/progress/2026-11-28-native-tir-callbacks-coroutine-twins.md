# native: the TIR lowering takes C callbacks and a coroutine's socket twins (D877)

docs/70 phase 4a part 10. A closure passed as a C callback, the closure as the userdata pointer and the userdata slot in either spelling now lower from TIR (`lowerCallback`, `CvClosureAsPointer`, `CvUserdataSlot` in `llvm_tir.l`), and a coroutine calling `hostAccept`/`hostRead`/`hostWrite` awaits the async twin in place. The census loses three callback fallbacks (`tryStartTask`, `rtThreadSpawnDetached`) and three coroutine-extern fallbacks; remaining native roots are `TsDefer`, `List[Unit]`/`Dictionary[String, Unit]` locals, `String.normalize` and callbacks with a by-value record. Covered by `scripts/ci/native-via-tir/callbacks.l` (AST and TIR output equal, AddressSanitizer clean) and the census suites.

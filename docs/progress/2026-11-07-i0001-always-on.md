# I0001 is always on (#8613)

- The last classes of silent `TyError` got rules or a named kind (D359): the open
  hole `tyErrorOpen`, extern `.new` over a same-named Lyric type, host constants
  and property reads, `externAsObject` of an unchecked operand, protected-type
  field reads in contracts, generic distinct constructors, and universal method
  names at other arities.
- The gate is the always-on error I0001 (D385); `LYRIC_TYERR_GATE` and W0036 are
  gone, and `scripts/ci/library-tyerr-census.sh`, `tyerr-census.sh` and
  `bootstrap.sh` count I0001.
- Two user-visible behaviour changes follow from it (D359): a member read through
  a package-qualified name no rule resolves (`P.missing`, another package's
  package-private config block) is T0020, and a member read off a primitive,
  `String` or slice that names none is T0113; `examples/jobqueue` read another
  package's config block and now goes through a `pub func pollIntervalMs()`.
- The two JVM source tests for the backend aborts J010 and J011 (`xs.bogus`,
  `n.bogus`) are replaced by a checker test, since no source reaches those aborts
  any more; the aborts stay as defence in depth.
- `externAsObject` on the JVM boxes a primitive operand the checker leaves
  unchecked (`Float.parseFloat`); before, it emitted the primitive unboxed.

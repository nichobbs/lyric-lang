# Await hoisting on TIR (docs/70 phase 3c, D504)

`Lyric.Tir.AwaitHoist` binds every operand up to and including an awaiting one to a fresh local ahead of the statement, so each `await` evaluates on an empty stack; conditional parts (short-circuit right sides, branches, guards, try bodies) keep their preludes inside them and a `while` condition with an `await` becomes a `loop`. `tir_await_hoist_self_test.l` builds small async packages, hoists them, checks the result verifies and that no call has an `await` behind another argument. `Pipeline.pipeHoistAwaitsTir` is the entry and `--verify-ir` prints an `I0003 hoist census`. This completes the four middle-end passes of phase 3c on TIR (monomorphisation D500, contract elaboration D502, `?` D503, await hoisting D504); the backends consume them in phase 4.

# lyric-unicode moves to Unicode 16.0 (#8497)

The UCD files and Unicode's conformance suites in lyric-unicode are now 16.0.0 and the tables are
regenerated with `tools/generate`. `Unicode.LineBreak` implements UAX #14 revision 53 (LB13 without IS,
LB15c and LB15d, LB19 narrowed, LB19a, LB20a, LB21a, the default LB25, LB10's properties), and passes
all 16672 lines of the 16.0 `LineBreakTest.txt`; `Unicode.Bidi` passes the 16.0 `BidiCharacterTest.txt`
unchanged (D411). The lyric-text, lyric-draw and native lyric-ui golden images are unchanged.
